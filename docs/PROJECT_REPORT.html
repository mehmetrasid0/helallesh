1. Executive Summary & Problem Definition

The Real-World Problem: University students and close friend groups constantly share resources: textbooks, hoodies, calculators, gaming gear, and split food/housing bills. However, asking for a borrowed item or small debt creates social awkwardness, leading to forgotten items, financial loss, and strained relationships.

The Helallesh Solution: Helallesh transforms awkward debt collection into lighthearted, gamified interaction through:

  • Bidirectional Balance Engine: Tracks both lent items/money (Verdim) and borrowed debts (Aldım) with an automatic Net Balance Hero banner.
  • Photo Proofing: Integrated camera/gallery photo upload for capturing receipt bills and item condition.
  • Mert Günaydın WhatsApp Tebligat Generator: Humorous, hyper-formal legal court summons sent natively via WhatsApp in one tap.
  • Helallik & Karma Score: A dynamic 0–100 punctuality index measuring friend trustworthiness.

4. Screen Gallery & User Flow

1. Dashboard / Ana Sayfa
Dynamic Net Balance Hero banner, quick filter pills (Tümü, Emanetler, Borçlar, Gecikenler), and color-coded transaction cards with directional tags.
2. Record Details & Tebligat Generator / Detay & Hatırlatıcı
Comprehensive view showing item details, photo proof thumbnail with full-screen zoom, debt settlement actions, and the Mert Günaydın comedic WhatsApp summon generator.
3. Add Transaction Form / Yeni Kayıt Aç
Interactive form with two-way direction selector ('Verdim / Aldım'), item/money toggle, friend picker, and camera/gallery photo proof upload.
4. Agalar Directory / Kişiler & Net Bakiye
Friends matrix showing individual net balances (Alacak/Borç), gamified trust badges, and single-tap full settlement button.
5. Helallik & Karma Analytics / Özet & Skor
Gamified 0-100 Helallik score, total lent capital vs. total debt breakdown, and punctuality velocity.
